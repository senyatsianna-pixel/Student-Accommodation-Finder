About Polokwane Student Living

Your Trusted Student Housing Partner in Limpopo

Polokwane Student Living was established to bridge the housing gap for students attending higher education institutions across Polokwane, including the University of Limpopo (Turfloop), Tshwane University of Technology (TUT) Polokwane Campus, Capricorn TVET College, and various private colleges.

Finding secure, comfortable, and affordable accommodation shouldn't be a stressful ordeal. Our curated directory features 39 verified residences ranging from peaceful single study rooms to budget-friendly sharing apartments and executive bachelors.

Our Core Commitments

NSFAS Compliance

We prioritize accredited housing options that accept direct bursary allowances, ensuring zero out-of-pocket setup worries.

Connectivity & Safety

Every listed residence guarantees high-speed uncapped Wi-Fi and 24/7 security features such as CCTV and biometric access.
